The slate mark, dialled down — three versions

Same idea in every one: a clapper stripe marks the pick. What changes is how loudly it says so. Each section shows the picked card with two ordinary ones under it, and the first section is exactly what is live right now for comparison.

Tonal — the blind emboss

Same rail, same shape, but cut in the card’s own paper tones instead of black. It reads as a texture pressed into the card — the way a watermark or a blind-embossed edge does on good stationery. You notice the card is different before you notice why.
