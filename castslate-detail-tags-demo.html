These are not new colours. They are the exact values the same two labels already use on your Browse page, shipped earlier tonight:

Why this is the version I'd stand behind. It isn't a taste judgement — it's removing a contradiction. Right now the identical badge is purple on the casting page and gold on the card that links to it, one click apart. Matching them costs nothing, removes the last cool block on the page, and leaves gold as the "we picked this" signal and green as the "live / paid" signal — two colours, each meaning one thing.

The one thing I would not do is invent a third warm colour for these tags. The point is that a badge should look the same everywhere it appears.
