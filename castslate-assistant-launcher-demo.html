Super Assistant launcher: three grown-up options

The bottom-right button is the one thing on every page, so it sets the tone for the whole site. Below are three replacements. Hover each one. Use “Replay entrance” to see how it arrives, and “Phone” to see it at phone size.

Why it reads as child’s play today: a shiny silver ball, a cartoon clapperboard, a drop-from-the-sky entrance that squashes and bounces, a clap every 10 seconds forever, and a springy hover. Each one is fine alone; together they feel like a toy.
The fix in all three: solid brand navy, a thin line icon, a quiet 0.45s fade-up entrance, no idle motion at all, and a hover that only deepens the colour or shows a label. Premium sites (Stripe, Linear, Apple Support) keep this button still and let the content move.

A · Concierge pill My pick

Navy pill with a thin spark icon and the words “Ask CastSlate”. A small green dot says “available”. It explains itself, so the “Try Super Assistant” nudge can be retired. On phones it shrinks to a plain round button.

B · Monogram disc

A navy circle with a thin gold hairline and a quiet speech mark. The label only appears on hover. It's the smallest footprint and the most “private members’ club” of the three.

C · Refined slate

Keeps the film idea but drops the cartoon: a thin line-drawn slate on a navy rounded square, completely still. On hover the arm lifts slightly, once. No clapping loop, no silver ball.
