## Part 2 — Softened framing copy (reduces "we vetted it" implication)

### A. Manager Mode page — Industry Events section
Add this sentence to the end of the events intro paragraph:

> These events are organized by independent third parties. CastSlate shares them for
> your convenience — we don't organize, vet, or endorse them, and you attend at your
> own discretion.

### B. Monthly Event email — footer disclaimer (replace the current line)
Current: *"Event details are set by the external host and can change — please confirm before you head out."*
Replace with:

> This event is run by an independent third party. CastSlate shares it for your
> convenience but doesn't organize, vet, or endorse it — details can change, so please
> confirm with the host, and attend at your own discretion.

### C. Optional — soften the email hero line
Current: *"…your CastSlate manager hand-picks one NYC event worth showing up for…"*
Optional softer version (less "vetted" implication, keeps energy):

> …we round up one notable NYC industry event worth knowing about…

*(Trade-off: "hand-picks/curated" reads more premium but leans toward implying vetting.
Addendum X §2 already says those words don't imply vetting, so this softening is
optional belt-and-suspenders.)*

## Notes for the attorney / before publishing
- Confirm governing-law/venue and whether an assumption-of-risk/release is enforceable
  as drafted in your governing state(s); some states narrow releases for gross
  negligence or as to certain claims.
- Consider a conspicuous "last updated / effective date" and user notice for material
  Terms changes; confirm users affirmatively accept Terms at signup.
- Consider general-liability insurance as the real-world backstop (Terms limit but do
  not eliminate exposure).
- Consider a light internal editorial standard for which events get promoted (avoid
  obviously high-risk / age-restricted / unvetted-organizer events).
