What this demo changes — click the card to open it

  • Opens as a slide-in panel, left → right, using the exact Browse-Castings sheet model (csSheetIn, 0.5s, same easing, same dim). Closes back out to the left.
  • "Other agencies" is gone. Its 249 entries are folded into Small / Mid-size / Major. 3 were duplicates of majors already listed (Paradigm LA, WME NY, IAG NY) and were merged out; IAG became the 8th Major. New totals: 570 companies — 248 small, 56 mid-size, 8 major, 258 management.

  • Copy: the roster footnote is deleted · "nothing to print" → "print a stack, write two lines on the back" · "You will not find it on ours." deleted · Major badge "No listed submission process" → "Typically signs through referrals" · card title now says Agency & Management.
