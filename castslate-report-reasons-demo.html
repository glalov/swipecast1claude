What actually makes the current list feel mean. Every option is a verdict about the other person — scam, impersonation, illegal. To choose one you have to accuse someone of it, and be sure. The rewrite moves each option to what the reporter noticed and adds the hedge — might, may, doesn't feel — so picking one is describing an impression rather than passing judgement.

The intro carries most of the weight. "We take action when our policies are violated" is legal-sounding and puts the burden on the reporter to have a case. "You don't need to be certain — if something feels off, telling us is enough" tells them the opposite, which is what you actually want: people report far less when they're worried about being wrong.

Where I deliberately stopped. I kept the header as Report this casting — that's the word people look for, and a friendlier heading would make the button harder to find in a bad moment. And I did not soften the under-18 option into vagueness; that one has to stay findable and unmistakable, so it only loses the clinical word "minor". Every stored value stays the same, so your admin queue and existing reports are untouched.
