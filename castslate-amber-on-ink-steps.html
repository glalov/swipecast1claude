Half the line was missing

You were right that the amber was hardly there, but width was not the whole reason. Only one of its two edges was ever doing any work.

Why 1.5px of amber disappeared

A thin line is read by its two edges. In the version you saw, the amber’s left edge sat against navy — 10.32×, about as strong as a boundary gets — and its right edge sat against the white card at 1.65×, which is close to nothing. So the line had one hard edge and one that dissolved, and a line with one edge reads as roughly half its actual width.

That is why simply making it thicker is the weaker fix: at 3px open you are still only getting one edge, just a wider smear of it. Closing the right side with a sliver of navy is worth more than a pixel of width, because it turns a 1.65× boundary into a 10.32× one. W2 and W3 below both do that; W1 is the thicken-only version so you can see the difference for yourself.

Where this lands

W2. It is the same 7.5px total as the version you already liked, the amber is only 0.5px wider than W1, and it reads clearly stronger than either because the whole line is finally working. Nothing about the character of the mark changes — it is still navy carrying the edge with a warm line in it, still no gradient, still no motion.
