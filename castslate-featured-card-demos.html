Option A

The slate rail

Your own logo, turned into an edge. A clapperboard stripe runs the full height of the card down the left side — vertical, so it survives scrolling, and unmistakably CastSlate rather than a generic accent. Nobody else can copy this without copying your mark.

Option B

The spotlight

No line, no badge chrome, no border colour. The card itself is lit — a warm wash falling from the top-left corner and a deeper shadow, so it sits forward of the flat white cards around it. The eye is caught by light and depth, which it notices faster than a coloured edge.
