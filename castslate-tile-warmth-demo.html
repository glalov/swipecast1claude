Only two tiles change. Film goes #2B3D80 navy → #9C4A22 rust, and Animation goes #4B3A8C purple → #8A3A52 wine. Their pale backing tints move with them.

Film matters most because it's the most common type on your board — Experimental, Independent and Student all share that tile, so the navy was repeating down the whole page.

Why the other four stay. Commercial amber and Print brown are already warm. Teal and green are worth keeping cool: they're the same two colours your live badge and pay chip use, so they read as a system rather than decoration — and with four warm tiles, they now provide the contrast instead of dominating.

On the wine. Animation needed to stay distinguishable from rust and from Commercial's amber, so it moves to the warm side of red rather than into the orange family. It's the one value here I'd call a judgement call rather than an obvious call.
