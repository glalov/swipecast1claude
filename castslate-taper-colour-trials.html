The rule teal breaks

The card has one standing rule that has survived every revision: the teal View Roles button is the only saturated element. It is why the pay chip is a tint instead of a filled green badge, and why the pick has always been gold rather than brand colour. A teal rule breaks it.

Two things soften that. It is 2px and tapered, so it is nowhere near the button’s visual weight; and the earlier teal rejection was a 3px bar across the top, thrown out for reading as a divider between cards — a position problem, not a colour one. So teal on the left edge has never actually been tested.

My honest read: the rule exists to stop the eye being pulled to two places at once, and on a page of ten cards a teal rule and a teal button will do exactly that. But you built the rule, so you get to spend it.

Verdict — 1, Pick Ink. It removes a colour instead of adding one.
