Five of the six are already literal and already good. Feature Film is a clapperboard. Short Film is a film strip. Independent Film is a film reel. Documentary is a camcorder. Student Film is a graduation cap, which is the right idea — it distinguishes student work from the rest rather than repeating "film" a fifth time.

Only Experimental Film is broken — the circle, triangle and rectangle. Replacing it with the projector fixes the one bad icon and keeps all six types distinguishable from each other.

Why I would not drop my six options in. Three of them already exist here under different names: my "film strip" is Short Film, my "reel" is Independent Film, my "clapperboard" is Feature Film. Adding them again would give you duplicate icons across different types, and an actor could no longer tell a feature from a short at a glance.
