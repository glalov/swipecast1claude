Fuller card, less story

No fades, no “read more” line. The card gets fuller by carrying different information — the roles and the specs — instead of more of the plot. Real rows, real roles, real rates from your database.

1 · Today. Hook line plus the story sentence. The card is mostly prose, and every word of it reappears when the casting opens.
2 · Roles preview — my pick. The story sentence comes out; the first three roles go in, with age range, gender and rate. The card is fuller than today and reveals less of the film, because roles are information the synopsis never carried. An actor can now find themselves on the card — “Female, 22–32, $300/day” — which is the actual decision. The remaining roles, every description, shoot days, media and audition requirements sit behind the click.
3 · Spec bar. No roles; a four-cell production block instead — shoot length, dates, audition method, submission requirements. Reads like a call sheet and fills the card with facts a producer would give you. Weaker than 2 for one reason: it tells an actor about the job, not about their part in it.
4 · Both. Spec bar and roles together. The most substantial card of the set — and honestly close to the ceiling before it starts to feel like the detail page. Worth trying if you want the board to look dense and professional; watch that only three or four listings now fit a screen.

What stays behind the click in tabs 2–4: the full synopsis, every role's written description, per-role shoot days, the audition and prescreen requirements per role, the schedule, the shoot location, and the remaining roles. That is a genuinely different payload from the card — nobody has to be teased into opening it.

Note on the role names. “Rowan Vale”, “Janice Huang” — these are character names, so on their own they tell an actor nothing. On the card the line under the name is doing all the work (Lead · Female · 22–32 · $300/day). If you prefer, the card can lead with the part instead of the character name — “Lead, female, 22–32” with the character name shown only after opening.
