Recommended opening

Charge, flash, and the light throws the curtain down

One move, not three. The cube winds up, releases a flash that blooms into the black, and the curtain drops carrying that light along its leading edge — so the flash reads as the thing that caused the reveal. Real speed by default; total length 2000ms, unchanged from today.

The wind-up is doing most of the work. A flash with no contraction before it is just a brightness change; the eye needs the pull-back to read the release as an event. And the blade of light on the curtain's edge is what ties the two halves together — without it you have a flash, then a transition. With it you have one gesture.

Ship it once per session, not every load. This is built to be the thing someone tells a friend about, and that only survives if it is rare. Full sequence on first arrival; on later loads inside the same session, skip straight to the page.
