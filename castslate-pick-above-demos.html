Demo G

The rubric

Nothing on the card changes at all. A small letterspaced line sits above it, out on the page, with a hairline running off to the right — the way a magazine names a section before the article starts. It marks the card without decorating it, and it is the only one of these that can carry a reason: “Picked — 8 roles, casting this week”.
