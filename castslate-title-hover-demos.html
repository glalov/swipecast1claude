Clear of the descenders

The rule was sitting 5px under the baseline, and a 27px “g” reaches 4.9px — so it landed exactly on the tail. It is now placed in em, not pixels, so it clears the descenders by the same proportion at every title size on the site. Below: the fix measured, then the four levels again. All still colour on hover — no movement, no JavaScript, nothing extra downloaded.

Already live, separately: clicking or tapping a casting title now opens that casting — at every screen width, including phones, where the title used to do nothing at all. That shipped on its own; the colour below is still yours to choose.

Weight on bad wifi: between 120 and 260 bytes of CSS inside a stylesheet that is already downloaded — no new request, no image, no font, no JavaScript, and no work at all until a pointer is over a card. Colour, underline and the wash all repaint text only: no reflow, no layout shift, no scroll jank.
Bug surface: every hover rule sits behind @media (hover:hover) and (pointer:fine), so a phone or tablet can never get stuck showing a hovered title after a tap — the classic hover bug. The same treatment is mirrored on :focus-visible for keyboard users, and prefers-reduced-motion drops the 150ms fade to an instant switch.

Whichever you pick applies to .cc-title, shared by Browse and the Featured Castings coverflow, so one rule covers both. Closed and archived cards keep their inert, dimmed titles.
