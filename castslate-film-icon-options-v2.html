All six are literal film objects. Nothing here is a metaphor: a strip of 35mm stock, a spool, a camera, a clapperboard, a projector, a vertical strip. Any of them is more accurate than what's live, because what's live depicts no object at all.

Still my pick: 1 · Film strip. Perforations down both edges are the one visual detail that says "film" and cannot say anything else. Chunky enough to hold at 28px, and horizontal, so it doesn't get confused with the Web Series monitor.

Runner up: 5 · Projector. It's what the current icon was reaching for — reel, body, beam — but drawn as one connected object so the idea actually lands.

6 · Vertical strip is the most graphic and fills the square tile best. Worth a look if you want the tiles to feel more designed than illustrative.
