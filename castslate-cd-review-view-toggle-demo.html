Reviewing headshots — your choice of Swipe or Grid

This is a preview of a new setting for casting-director accounts. Every CD would get the toggle in the top-right of their review screen and could switch between the one-at-a-time Swipe deck (today's default) and a scrollable Grid of all headshots — whichever they prefer. Their choice is remembered per account. Try the toggle, swipe or click the buttons, and move actors between folders. Nothing here is real data.

↓ Use the toggle in the top-right to switch views. Both views share the same shortlist.
