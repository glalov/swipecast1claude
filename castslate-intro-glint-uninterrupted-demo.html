Glint before the curtain

You were right — the glint was crossing into the curtain and getting clipped. The mark only survives about 275ms into the fall before the curtain's edge wipes through it, so a 460ms glint starting at 1330ms was always going to lose its tail. The fix isn't a slower curtain, it's an earlier glint: run it straight out of the spin, let it finish, hold a beat, then drop. Same effect, same total length.
