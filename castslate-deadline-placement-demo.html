What changed, exactly

Three edits on this page and nothing else:
  • The countdown waits until 3 days. Above that the status strip says nothing about time — same rule the browse card already follows. At 3 days and under, 3 days left to apply appears in red, and the final-48h ticking clock is untouched.
  • The date is stated once. The DEADLINE fact row and the closes Oct 14, 2026 under the Apply button both go; the countdown line no longer repeats the date either.
  • It moves up and gets quiet. A small grey CLOSES OCT 14, 2026 above the card, the way Mandy puts EXPIRES: September 23, 2026 at the top of the page — present for anyone who needs it, invisible to anyone who doesn't. It turns red only in the last 3 days, and reads CLOSED OCT 14, 2026 once the casting has finished.
